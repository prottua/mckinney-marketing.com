ABOUT STRIKE

Marketing should work for the business behind it.

I believe good marketing starts with understanding people — your customers, their needs, and what makes them choose one business over another.

It isn’t about being everywhere, using every platform, or spending more just because everyone else is doing it. It’s about finding the opportunities that make sense for your business and turning them into real growth.

That’s the idea behind Strike: marketing with a purpose, a clear direction, and a real understanding of the business it serves.

WHAT GUIDES MY WORK

Values that make a difference.

Purpose over noise

I focus on marketing activities that support real business goals, not trends for their own sake.

Every dollar matters

Thoughtful testing, realistic expectations, and investing where there is a clear reason to expect value.

Direct communication

You work directly with me. No unnecessary layers, confusing explanations, or wondering who’s making the decisions.

Strategy and execution

A strategy is only useful when it can be put into practice. I connect the big picture with the technical details needed to make it happen.

EXPERIENCE & APPROACH

Understanding people. Connecting strategy with execution.

I bring more than 10 years of experience in product development and digital marketing.

One of my greatest strengths is understanding what people need and translating that understanding into practical solutions. In product development, that means connecting user behavior and business goals with technical requirements. In marketing, the same thinking helps connect customer needs with the right message, channel, and experience.

I’ve built strategies and led their execution with a team of 10 people, working across the gap between big-picture thinking and the technical details needed to make things happen.
